# Sóc de Poble: The Grassroots Architecture Report 🏺🚀

## Vision: Sovereign Digital Ruralism

Sóc de Poble is not a standard SaaS (Software as a Service). It is a **Village Cell Infrastructure** designed to empower rural communities through technology that is physically and legally owned by its users.

### The "Village Cell" Concept
Instead of a monolithic cloud, we propose a decentralized network of independent nodes. Each town maintains its own data residency, ensuring that the memory of the village stays in the village.

### Key Pillars
- **Sovereign Sovereignty**: You own your data, in spite of the cloud [Source 79].
- **Calm Technology**: Systems that respect human attention and rural rhythms [Source 104].
- **Iron Truth**: A notarial approach to data through the IAIA Librarian and verifiable citations.
