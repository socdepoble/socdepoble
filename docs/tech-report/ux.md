# UX/UI: Ergonomics for the Rural Field 🌾🧤

Technology in the village must work in the "bancal" (field), not just in the office.

## 1. Bancal-Ready Visuals
Our design system is audited against **MIL-STD-1472G** standards [Source 103] for high-contrast visibility.
- **Sunlight Mode**: High-contrast, large typography, and specialized color palettes that remain readable under direct sunlight (10,000 foot-candles).
- **Glassmorphism for Context**: Using depth and transparency to keep the user aware of their environment while interacting with the digital layer.

## 2. Calm Technology
Following the principles of Mark Weiser and Amber Case [Source 104], Sóc de Poble is designed to live in the periphery of attention.
- **Non-Intrusive Notifications**: Using subtle visual cues instead of heart-attack-inducing alerts.
- **Asynchronous Design**: The UI acknowledges the pace of rural life, where a task might be started in the field and finished at home.

## 3. Haptic Feedback & Eyes-Free
When hands are covered in soil or gloves, visual-only interfaces fail.
- **Haptic Confirmation**: Distinct vibration patterns for successful metadata entry or critical alerts [Source 3].
- **Voice-First Input**: Integration of Nano-Banana for hands-free data entry in the field.
